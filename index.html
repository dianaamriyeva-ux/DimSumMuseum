About these objects

In 2026, people across Toronto left cards and fortune symbols in a time capsule, hoping to pass their cultures on to us. A hundred years later, the capsules have been opened.

Many of these objects were once tied to money. Our grandparents lived through the end of that world, and rebuilt it around a simpler rule: take what you need, give what you can. We keep these objects because, read closely, most of them were never really about money. They were about giving.

We honor the ancestral lands of the Haudenosaunee Confederacy, the Métis, and Anishinaabek peoples.
