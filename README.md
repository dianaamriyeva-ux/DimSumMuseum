# DimSumMuseum — The Lake Museum

Toronto, 2126. After the old money economy collapsed, people rebuilt around one rule:
take what you need, give what you can. Time capsules sealed in 2026 (see the group's
Fortune Time Capsule page) have been found, opened and kept at the Lake Museum.

Open `index.html` in a browser; there is no build step.

- `js/objects.js` holds every object's text: history, the 2026 card, the 2126 meaning and sources. Edit text here.
- `js/museum.js` runs the entrance, the room and the close-up view.
- `css/style.css` holds the look. Fonts, grain and brass plates match the time capsule page.
- `assets/objects/` holds the drawings. The red envelope, money toad, diya and mithai PNGs come from the
  Fortune Time Capsule (licensed Adobe Stock, IDs noted in `js/objects.js`); the mati and vasilopita coin were drawn for this page.
